# About Symmetra Systems — public positioning

Source for the main page (`src/components/OnboardingScreen.jsx`). The underlying brief,
*SRG – Symmetra Systems Design Studio: business case and delivery plan*, is an **internal**
document and is not stored in this repository.

## What the public page says

- **Who:** the SRG – Symmetra Systems Design Studio, Systems Realization Group, Cranfield
  University. It turns systems-engineering research into decision-support tools that keep people in charge.
- **Goals:** trace the consequences of change; make human–AI decision roles explicit
  (AI-led / AI-supported / human-led *recommendations*); keep a defensible record of evidence,
  rationale and approval; validate with industry before making claims.
- **Principles:** decision support, not autonomous approval. Decision *allocation* is kept
  distinct from decision *authority*. Tools show sources, assumptions and uncertainty; demonstrations
  use synthetic or authorised data only; no connection to live industrial control systems.
- **Available now:** the Business Plan Co-Pilot ([workflow.md](workflow.md)).
- **In development:** *Symmetra Change Impact* — "Understand what an engineering change affects,
  identify who should decide, and retain a defensible record of the evidence and approval."
  Shown as an early-stage prototype, not yet available.

## What must not be published

The repository and site are public. Keep these out of the UI and docs unless the people and the
Centre have agreed to publish them:

- names of individuals, students or role-holders;
- funding sources, budgets, costs or internal dates and milestones;
- targets presented as results (no pilots, customers, savings or accuracy figures until validated);
- partner or prospect organisations.

Wording should describe intent and status ("in development", "being validated"), not outcomes.
